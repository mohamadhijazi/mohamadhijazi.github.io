Voice export in Edge/Chrome (100% in-browser, no server needed):
1. Choose a narrator Voice and export format (MP4 or WEBM).
2. Click Export Video + Voice.
3. In the sharing prompt, choose Entire screen (or Screen 1).
4. Important: Enable the checkbox “Also share system audio” at the bottom-left so the narrator voice is captured.
5. Click Share. The 1080p canvas video and narrator voice will record and download automatically when finished.
