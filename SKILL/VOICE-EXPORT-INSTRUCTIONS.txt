VOICE EXPORT IN MICROSOFT EDGE

1. Run start-server.bat.
2. Open http://localhost:8080.
3. Choose a narrator voice.
4. Click Export Video + Narrator.
5. In the sharing window choose This tab.
6. Turn on Share tab audio.
7. Click Share and keep the tab active until export completes.

The downloaded WEBM includes the Canvas animation and native browser narrator.
