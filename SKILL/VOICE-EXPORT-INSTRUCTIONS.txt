VOICE EXPORT IN MICROSOFT EDGE & GOOGLE CHROME (100% IN-BROWSER)

1. Run start-server.bat (or open index.html directly in Edge or Chrome).
2. Choose a narrator voice from the dropdown.
3. Select your desired export format:
   - "MP4 Video (.mp4)" (Recommended, high-quality H.264 video + AAC audio)
   - "WEBM Video (.webm)"
4. Click "Export Video + Voice".
5. In the sharing prompt:
   - Select "Entire screen" (or Screen 1).
   - IMPORTANT: Check the box "Also share system audio" at the bottom-left.
     (This captures the browser's native narrator voice directly from system audio).
   - Click "Share".
6. Keep the browser tab visible until recording finishes.

The downloaded MP4 or WEBM video will automatically contain the crisp 1080p Canvas animation and the synchronized narrator voice!
