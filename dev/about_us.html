Security-first usage

Use the application securely from day one.

Impact360 is designed to support safe usage with a clear operational model and secure access habits.

How to use it safely

  • ✓Access the platform only from trusted devices and secure browser sessions.
  • ✓Use role-based permissions so employees only see information relevant to their work.
  • ✓Review updates, risks, and ownership changes regularly to maintain operational awareness.
  • ✓Keep passwords strong, use multi-factor authentication where available, and log out after shared use.
  • ✓Verify data before making strategic changes, especially around financial, project, and risk decisions.
