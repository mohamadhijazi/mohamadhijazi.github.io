# TOGAF Standard, 10th Edition Exam Study Kit

> **Audience:** Intermediate enterprise/solution architects preparing for TOGAF Enterprise Architecture Foundation and Practitioner.
>
> **Scope:** TOGAF Standard, 10th Edition certification path only. This kit deliberately avoids TOGAF 9-specific terminology and exam objectives unless a difference is explicitly identified.

## Important naming clarification

The official certifications are named **TOGAF Enterprise Architecture Foundation** and **TOGAF Enterprise Architecture Practitioner**, not “TOGAF 10 Foundation” and “TOGAF 10 Practitioner”. They are based on the TOGAF Standard, 10th Edition.

## Exam routes

- **OGEA-101, Part 1:** Foundation, 40 multiple-choice questions, 60 minutes, 60% pass mark.
- **OGEA-102, Part 2:** Practitioner, 8 scenario questions, 90 minutes, gradient scoring, 60% pass mark, integrated open-book reference.
- **OGEA-103:** Combined Part 1 and Part 2, 48 questions, 150 minutes.
- **OGEA-10B:** Bridge route for eligible TOGAF 9 Certified holders.

Always re-check current rules before booking.

## How to use this kit

- For **Part 1**, prioritize exact distinctions, ADM purpose/order, terminology, deliverable-versus-artifact distinctions, and governance.
- For **Part 2**, practice selecting the **best TOGAF-aligned action in context**. The correct answer usually respects governance, stakeholder concerns, traceability, iteration, requirements, and the agreed architecture process.
- Do not memorize isolated words. Learn the relationship: **stakeholder concern → viewpoint → view → architecture description → decision → governed change**.
- Create a one-page “error log” from every practice session.

## Ethical and copyright note

This is an original study aid. It does not reproduce the official standard, paid study guides, or recalled live exam questions. “Exam experience” guidance is limited to legitimate test-taking patterns and public exam structure, not dumps.

## Readiness target

You are ready when you can:

- explain each ADM phase’s purpose and likely outcomes without prompts;
- distinguish principles, requirements, constraints, concerns, views, viewpoints, building blocks, artifacts, and deliverables;
- solve scenario questions by eliminating answers that skip governance, stakeholder engagement, requirements traceability, or transition planning;
- consistently score at least **80%** on fresh Part 1 practice and explain why each rejected Part 2 option is weaker.
