## Version 10 guardrails

- Use the official certification names: **Enterprise Architecture Foundation** and **Enterprise Architecture Practitioner**.
- Study the modular structure: **Fundamental Content plus Series Guides**.
- Do not rely on TOGAF 9-only mock exams as your main source. Some enduring concepts overlap, but exam outcomes, emphasis, structure, and names differ.
- Validate any third-party course against **X2202**.
- Practitioner is about applying and analyzing, not recalling page text.

## What this kit does not claim

- It is not an official publication or accredited course.
- It does not include confidential exam questions or dumps.
- It is not a substitute for the licensed official standard or official practice tests.
- Exam policies can change. Re-check official sources before booking.
