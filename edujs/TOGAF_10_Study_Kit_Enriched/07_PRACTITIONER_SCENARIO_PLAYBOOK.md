# 7. Practitioner Scenario Playbook

## 7.1 The seven-pass method

For each OGEA-102 scenario:

1. **Locate the phase:** What is currently happening?
2. **Identify the decision:** What does the question ask the architect to do?
3. **Identify stakeholders and concerns:** Who must accept or govern the outcome?
4. **Find the missing discipline:** scope, requirement, view, gap, risk, roadmap, contract, compliance?
5. **Respect sequence:** Do not solve downstream details before upstream authorization and requirements.
6. **Rank options:** complete and aligned beats partially correct.
7. **Check tailoring:** prefer an answer proportionate to context, not ceremonial bureaucracy.

## 7.2 Quality hierarchy for answers

A high-quality answer usually:

- directly addresses the stated concern;
- uses the appropriate ADM phase or technique;
- engages accountable stakeholders;
- uses/reuses relevant repository assets;
- maintains traceability to requirements and principles;
- assesses impacts and risk;
- follows governance and updates controlled outputs.

A medium-quality answer may use a valid technique but omit stakeholders, traceability, or follow-through.

A weak answer often:

- jumps to a product or vendor;
- ignores current phase and authority;
- creates documentation without a decision purpose;
- treats architecture as only technology;
- bypasses governance because of schedule pressure;
- assumes every change requires a full new ADM cycle;
- assumes every deviation is automatically rejected.

## 7.3 Scenario archetypes

### Stakeholder conflict

Best pattern: analyze power/interest and concerns, select viewpoints, facilitate decision, document resolution in requirements and governed architecture.

### Executive asks for rapid vision

Best pattern: focus on Phase A outcomes, value, scope, stakeholders, readiness, risk, and authorization. Do not attempt exhaustive B-D detail.

### Vendor solution already selected

Best pattern: test the selection against requirements, principles, target capability, risk, and conformance. Do not automatically accept or reject it.

### Multiple implementation dependencies

Best pattern: consolidate gaps, define work packages and Transition Architectures in E, then prioritize and resource in F.

### Delivery team deviates

Best pattern: use Architecture Contract/compliance review, assess impact, remediate or approve a governed dispensation, and update controlled assets.

### Major external change

Best pattern: Phase H monitoring and impact assessment, then determine whether maintenance or a new cycle is warranted.

### Vague requirements

Best pattern: business scenarios and stakeholder engagement, followed by managed, traceable architecture requirements.

### Different business units need autonomy

Best pattern: federation/partitioning, explicit principles and decision rights, common interfaces and shared standards where enterprise value requires them.

## 7.4 Option elimination checklist

Reject or downgrade an answer when it:

- solves a different problem;
- confuses architecture with project management;
- uses Phase G controls during early visioning without reason;
- finalizes migration before target gaps and work packages are understood;
- omits risk or stakeholder acceptance for a consequential choice;
- treats the repository as classification rather than storage;
- treats the Enterprise Continuum as a database;
- confuses the Architecture Roadmap with the detailed Implementation and Migration Plan.

## 7.5 Open-book navigation strategy

Know where to look for:

- phase objectives and approach;
- stakeholder and business scenario techniques;
- content concepts;
- applying/tailoring guidance;
- governance and compliance.

Use searchable anchor terms rather than long natural-language queries. Examples: `Transition Architecture`, `Statement of Architecture Work`, `dispensation`, `gap analysis`, `viewpoint`.

## 7.6 Timing

- 1 minute: read question and identify ask.
- 4 minutes: analyze scenario.
- 3 minutes: rank options.
- 1 minute: reference lookup if needed.
- 1 minute: final check.

If stuck, select the option with best governance, sequence, and completeness, then move on.
