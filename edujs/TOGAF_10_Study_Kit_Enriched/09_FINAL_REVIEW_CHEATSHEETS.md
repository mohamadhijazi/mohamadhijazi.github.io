# 9. Final Review Cheat Sheets

## 9.1 ADM in one line each

- **Preliminary:** establish the architecture capability.
- **A:** agree vision, value, scope, stakeholders, and authorization.
- **B:** develop Business Architecture and gaps.
- **C:** develop Data and Application Architectures and gaps.
- **D:** develop Technology Architecture and gaps.
- **E:** shape solutions, work packages, Transition Architectures, initial roadmap.
- **F:** prioritize and finalize implementation/migration planning.
- **G:** govern implementation conformance.
- **H:** monitor and govern architecture change.
- **Requirements:** manage needs and traceability throughout.

## 9.2 Confusion pairs

- Repository **stores**; Continuum **classifies/contextualizes**.
- Viewpoint **defines conventions**; view **is the representation**.
- Deliverable **formal work product**; artifact **catalog/matrix/diagram**; building block **capability/component**.
- ABB **required capability**; SBB **implementation realization**.
- Roadmap **time-oriented work packages**; migration plan **detailed execution**.
- Phase E **package the change**; Phase F **plan and prioritize execution**.
- Phase G **govern implementation**; Phase H **govern ongoing change**.
- Principle **guides**; requirement **must be met**; constraint **limits**.

## 9.3 Scenario decision tree

1. Is capability or authority missing? → Preliminary.
2. Is the initiative not scoped/approved? → A.
3. Is a domain Baseline/Target/gap needed? → B/C/D.
4. Are increments and work packages needed? → E.
5. Is sequencing, value, cost, risk, or dependency prioritization needed? → F.
6. Is delivery deviating? → G and compliance governance.
7. Did the environment change? → H.
8. Are needs unclear or changing? → Requirements Management plus appropriate stakeholder technique.

## 9.4 Ten high-value exam rules

1. Start with stakeholder concerns and business outcomes.
2. Respect the current ADM phase.
3. Reuse before creating from scratch.
4. Tailor method and content to decision needs.
5. Maintain requirements traceability.
6. Think across all architecture domains.
7. Use Transition Architectures for manageable increments.
8. Put exceptions through governance.
9. Balance value, risk, dependency, and readiness.
10. Choose the most complete, context-fit answer, not merely a true statement.
